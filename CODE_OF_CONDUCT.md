# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Bullgate community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing views,
giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes,
and focusing on what benefits the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, harassment,
trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct and will communicate
moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when a person officially represents
the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
`marco@torphi.com.br` with the subject `Code of Conduct - Bullgate`.

Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly,
with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders may apply a private correction, a formal warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban according to the severity, duration, and pattern of the
behavior. Retaliation or unwanted contact after an enforcement action may lead
to a stronger consequence.
